Retail Sales Analytics

Where does a $2.75B retail business make its money, and what really drives its sales?

  • Power BI

What I found

  • Revenue follows campaign activity. Daily revenue moved with the number of live campaigns (r = 0.75), and there were no sales at all on the 6 days with no campaign running.
  • A bigger budget did not buy more revenue. Every campaign brought in between $54.3M and $55.7M, whatever its budget, so timing mattered more than spend.
  • Q3 was the strongest quarter at 34.2% of the year, and October the peak month ($422.89M).

I also cut the model from 39.8 MB to 14.9 MB by removing columns and measures the report never used, without changing a single number on the dashboard.

Power BI · Telecom

Customer Retention Analytics

Why are telecom customers leaving, who is leaving, and what does it cost?

  • Power BI
  • DAX
  • Power Query
  • Churn analysis
Customers
7,043
Churn rate
26.5%
Lost to churn
$139,131/mo

What I found

  • Contract type is the strongest driver. Month-to-month customers churned at 45.8% against 2.5% on two-year contracts, and made up 88.6% of all churners.
  • Churn happens early. 55.5% of churners left within their first 12 months.
  • Competitors, not price, win the customers. 45.0% left for a competitor; only 11.3% left over price.

As an accountant, my first question after the churn rate was "what does it cost?" The answer: $139,131 a month, about $1.67M a year.
